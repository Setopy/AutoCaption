Questions

Is it really free?

Yes — completely. No trial, no watermark, no account, no catch. It costs nothing to run because your own device does the work.

Where does my video go?

Nowhere. The video, the audio, and the transcription all stay in your browser. You can even turn off your internet after the page loads and it still works.

Why does it want Chrome on a computer?

The captioning uses modern browser features that work most reliably in Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop. Keep the tab visible while it renders — it processes in real time, so a 1-minute video takes about a minute.

What if it hears a word wrong?

Speech recognition isn't perfect, especially with unusual names or heavy background noise. Clear audio gives the best results — you can always run the video again.

What languages does it support?

It auto-detects the spoken language and supports dozens of languages.
